HardwareSensors.exe is the System Bio Mon sensor helper. Its source and
build.ps1 are in app/sensors in the application source tree. It uses the
Windows .NET Framework and the unmodified libraries shipped in the official
LibreHardwareMonitor v0.9.6 release.
